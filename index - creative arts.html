Teacher setup: make the email fully automatic (optional)

This page uses a mailto: link, so the learner's own email app opens with the result already filled in for . The teacher address is the TEACHER_EMAIL value at the top of the script. To send silently without opening an email app, connect a service such as EmailJS or Formspree inside emailTeacher(). Data stays in memory only; nothing is saved in the browser.
